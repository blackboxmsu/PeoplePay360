1.	Problem	Understanding
A.	The	Real-World	Problem
Most	basic	HR	tools	treat	employee	data,	attendance,	leave,	and	salary	as	separate,	disconnected	records.	In	reality,	payroll	is	a	
derived
process:	it	depends	on	which	contract	is	active	for	an	employee	in	a	given	period,	what	schedule	that	employee	works,	whether	their
attendance	has	exceptions,	and	what	leave	balances	they've	consumed.	A	tool	that	only	offers	CRUD	screens	for	these	entities	without
connecting	them	fails	the	moment	payroll	has	to	be	run,	because	payroll	math	needs	all	of	them	to	agree.
Who	Experiences	This	Problem
HR	Managers
	ΓÇö	juggle	employee	lifecycle	events	(new	hires,	contract	renewals,	transfers)	without	a	single	source	of	truth.
Payroll	Officers/Managers
	ΓÇö	must	manually	reconcile	attendance,	leave,	and	contract	data	before	running	payroll,	which	is	slow	and
error-prone.
Employees
	ΓÇö	lack	visibility	into	their	own	attendance,	leave	balance,	and	payslip	history.
Finance/Admin	leadership
	ΓÇö	need	aggregated,	real-time	visibility	into	salary	cost,	headcount,	and	payroll	risk,	not	static
spreadsheets.
Why	the	Problem	Exists
HR	systems	are	often	built	module-by-module	without	shared	foreign	keys	(Employee	Γåö	Contract	Γåö	Attendance	Γåö	Time	Off	Γåö	Payslip).
Payroll	rules	(structures/rules,	sequencing,	formulas)	are	often	hardcoded	instead	of	configurable,	so	every	salary	change	needs	a	code
change.
Contract	history	isn't	period-aware	ΓÇö	systems	let	two	contracts	be	"active"	at	once,	which	breaks	payroll	calculation	for	that	period.
Leave	balances	aren't	automatically	deducted	from	allocations	when	a	request	is	approved,	causing	manual	balance	tracking.
Current	Pain	Points	/	Inefficiencies
No	unified	employee	"hub"	that	links	to	related	records	(contracts,	attendance,	leave).
Payroll	teams	manually	cross-check	attendance	and	leave	data	before	computing	salary.
Salary	rules	are	static/hardcoded	rather	than	configurable	and	sequenced.
No	visibility	into	payroll	issues	(missing	bank	info,	duplicate	payslips)	before	finalization.
Dashboards,	where	they	exist,	use	static/mock	numbers	instead	of	live	aggregated	data.

Key	Business	Objectives
Period-accurate	contract	selection	for	payroll	(no	concurrent	"Running"	contracts	for	the	same	employee/period).
Configurable,	sequenced	Salary	Rules	that	actually	drive	payslip	line	items	(not	static	mockups).
